A plugin for Hermes Agent

Your own small model, learned from your work.

Tironian watches the work you already do with Hermes, trains a small model on the tasks you keep repeating, tests it on your own past sessions, and only then lets it answer. When it isn't sure, your usual model answers and you never notice.

Read the docs D

Free and MIT licensed. The first report needs no keys, no GPU and costs nothing.

Get started in Hermes
$hermes plugins install angelraph/tironian
What does this do?

Hermes downloads the plugin from GitHub and adds it to your plugins. Nothing runs until you enable it with hermes plugins enable tironian. It installs no packages: the part that runs inside Hermes uses only the Python standard library.

How it works

Three things, done carefully.

01Learn

It learns from what went well

Every step is recorded with secrets masked, and graded by what actually happened. Only good turns from your own model are learned from.

Capture and grading → 02Prove

It has to pass a test first

A small model per task, tested on sessions it never saw. It goes live only above an 85% lower confidence bound.

Training and the gate → 03Answer

It answers, with a safety net

Every answer is checked before Hermes sees it. Anything off, and your usual model replies instead.

Routing and fallback →
Why it gets better every week

A loop, not a one-off.

Each week of normal use adds graded examples. Families that were too small become trainable, and models that failed their test get another chance with more data.

Capture

Why did it answer like that?

Every request, model step and tool result, masked and stored on your machine. The raw material for everything that follows.

Mark what worked. Flag what broke.

Deterministic signals from the turn itself, plus your own /tironian good and bad. When it is unclear how a turn went, it is not learned from.

Train

A small model per task.

Qwen3 1.7B, LoRA, two epochs, loss on the answer only. The prompt it learns from is byte for byte what it will be served.

Training in detail
base     unsloth/Qwen3-1.7B
method   LoRA r16, all linear layers
output   Q4_K_M GGUF, about 1.1 GB
cost     about $0.15 on a Modal L4
Test

Ship only what passes.

Same tool, matching arguments, valid format. The pass rate's lower confidence bound has to clear your threshold before anything changes.

Own your model

Built by an engineer, for people who want control.

Private by default

Nothing leaves your computer until you start a training run, and then only to your own Modal account.

Never worse than today

Your model is called at most once per request, and any problem in Tironian falls through to it.

Standard formats

Training data in Nous's Hermes function calling format. Models as plain GGUF files you can run anywhere.
