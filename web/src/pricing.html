What you save

The other side of the ledger.

Every request a small model answers is one your paid model doesn't. Tironian counts it: each served request is credited with the average cost of that task on your own model, and the total shows on the Desktop page and in /tironian. If your model is free, the saving is time and privacy rather than money, and Tironian shows that honestly as $0.

Do I need a card for Modal?

No. Modal's Starter plan gives a small free credit without a card, enough for a couple of runs. Adding a card raises the monthly free credit; you are only charged above it, and you can set a spending limit in Modal.

Can a run cost more than the estimate?

Not more than your cap. max_usd_per_run becomes the GPU job's timeout, so the job is stopped before it can cost more. The default cap is $1.00.

What does running the model cost?

Nothing extra. The trained model runs on your own computer through Hermes's local model runtime.
